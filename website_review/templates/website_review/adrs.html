Accessing this service

Referrals through your ADRS worker.

This particular service accepts referrals directly from an ADRS worker.

Speak to your ADRS worker about a referral for a 12-week planned block of respite sitting. Referrals are offered city-wide, including for kinship families affected by a loved one’s alcohol or drug misuse.
