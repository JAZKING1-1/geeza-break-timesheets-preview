A little help with your timesheet

  1. Choose the date from the calendar and select the service / work type. Use one row for each shift. To work in another month, open Different month or year? above the entries. You can leave unused rows empty.
  2. Enter your start, finish and unpaid break. Use 24-hour times: for example, 09:00 to 17:00. Add a short purpose if helpful.
  3. Check, then save or submit. Save draft lets you make more changes later. Submit timesheet sends your completed month for review.

For an overnight shift, open Options in that row and choose the finish date. To remove a row, tick its Remove box and save. Untick it before saving to undo.
